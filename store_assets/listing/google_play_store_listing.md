## 3. Full Description (Max 4,000 characters)

Never miss your train or bus again. **EkiBoard** brings official concourse-grade live departure boards, interactive home screen widgets, Android 16 Live Updates, a **Wear OS** wrist companion, and an on-device **AI Virtual Stationmaster** directly to your Android phone, foldable, tablet, and watch.

Built with **Material 3 Expressive** design, EkiBoard delivers instant, clutter-free countdown predictions across **5 major global transit regions**:

🌍 **SUPPORTED REGIONS & TRANSIT NETWORKS**
• **🇬🇧 United Kingdom (London & National):**
  – **Transport for London (TfL):** London Underground (all 11 Tube lines), Elizabeth line, London Overground (*Liberty, Lioness, Mildmay, Suffragette, Weaver, Windrush*), DLR, London Trams & London Buses.
  – **National Rail (Darwin):** Live departure boards, platform predictions, 4/8/12-coach formations, split/join portion associations & fast/semi-fast calling points across UK rail operators (Thameslink, Chiltern, LNER, GWR, SWR, Southeastern, c2c & more).
  – **UK National Buses (BODS):** Real-time bus arrival predictions.
• **🇭🇰 Hong Kong:**
  – **MTR & Light Rail:** Live next-train arrivals across all 10 MTR heavy rail lines and Light Rail routes.
  – **Franchised Buses:** Unified real-time ETAs across KMB, LWB, Citybus (CTB), and New Lantao Bus (NLB) with full bilingual (English / Traditional Chinese) support.
• **🇸🇬 Singapore:**
  – **MRT & LRT:** North-South, East-West, North East, Circle, Downtown & Thomson-East Coast MRT lines plus LRT networks with official station codes.
  – **LTA Buses:** Live arrival countdowns, crowding load indicators & vehicle types (`SD`, `DD`, `BD`).
• **🇦🇺 Australia (Sydney & NSW):**
  – **Transport for NSW:** Sydney Metro (`M1`), Sydney Trains (`T1`–`T9`), Intercity lines & Sydney Ferries (`F1`–`F9`).
• **🇯🇵 Japan (Tokyo):**
  – **Tokyo Metro, Toei Subway & JR East:** All 9 Tokyo Metro lines, 4 Toei lines & major JR East commuter lines (*Yamanote, Chuo Rapid, Keihin-Tohoku, Saikyo, Shonan-Shinjuku* & more) with alphanumeric station codes (`G01`, `JY01`) and bilingual names.

✨ **KEY FEATURES**

🔴 **Real-Time Departures & Adaptive Backlog Alerts**
• Live minute countdowns paired with exact scheduled clock times (`22:14`).
• Live vehicle progression indicators (*At Platform*, *Approaching*, *In Transit*) and coach length badges.
• **Platform Backlog Detection:** Automatically compares live gaps against scheduled headways to warn you when trains are bunching or delayed.
• **Instant Line & Direction Filtering:** Tap any colored line pill to filter by line or cycle through directions (*Northbound*, *Southbound*, *Eastbound*, *Westbound*).
• **Last Train Home & 24hr Night Service:** Automatic late-night indicators for final departures and Night Tube / Night Overground services.

🤖 **EkiBoard AI — Virtual Stationmaster**
• Powered by **On-Device Gemini Nano** (Android ML Kit GenAI) with cloud fallback via **Firebase AI Logic**.
• Ask natural-language commute questions (*"Any delays on the Bakerloo line?"*, *"Next fast train from Marylebone"*), inspect live network disruptions, and listen to hands-free **Text-to-Speech** concourse announcements.

🔔 **Android 16 Live Updates & Per-Stop Alighting Alerts**
• Pin any train or bus to your status bar and lock screen with **Promoted Ongoing Notifications**.
• See a live status bar countdown chip (`4 min`, `1 min`, `Due`) and get alerted 1 stop before your destination or when a platform changes (`Plat TBD ➔ Plat 11`).

⚡ **Interactive Home Screen Widgets (Glance)**
• Pin live station boards to your home screen with per-widget line & direction filtering, manual refresh, and background disruption notifications when your pinned commute lines are delayed.

⌚ **Wear OS Companion App — Built for the Concourse**
• **Full-Screen Concourse Sprint & Alighting HUD:** Automatically launches when tracking a train—featuring a giant `PLAT X` hero badge, live `MM:SS` countdown timer, outer bezel progress ring, and on-watch calling-point selector for 1-stop alighting alerts.
• **Instant Wrist Haptic & Wake-Screen Alerts:** Distinct vibration patterns and native Wear OS notifications when a concourse platform is announced (`TBD ➔ Plat 11`), a platform changes (`⚠️ P9`), or your target stop is 1 station away.
• **Inline Calling Points, Coach Formations & Delay Repay:** Tap any departure to inspect coach counts (`10 coaches`) and calling points, plus automatic **Delay Repay (`💸 +15m+`)** eligibility badges.
• **Wrist AI Concourse Triage (Voice & Keyboard):** Ask questions by voice (`🎤`), swipe keyboard (`⌨️`), or 1-tap triage prompts (*Fastest Train*, *Delays & Delay Repay*, *Last Train*) with instant `📱 Continue in Phone AI` handoff.
• **On-Watch Station Search, Rotary Scroll & Ambient OLED Mode:** Add or search any station by voice/text directly on your watch, scroll with the rotary crown, and switch to burn-in-safe **Ambient OLED (`🌙`)** concourse mode.
• **Motion-Gated Concourse Sprint Polling:** Automatically boosts live refresh cadence when walking into your station perimeter and provides a GPS alighting geofence safety net.

📱 **Built for Foldables, Tablets & Desktops**
• **Adaptive 2-Pane & 3-Pane Navigation:** View your station dashboard, station detail, and live calling-point timeline side-by-side on unfolded displays, tablets, and desktop/ChromeOS windows.
• **Split-Flap & LED Desk Clock Mode:** Half-fold your device into **Tabletop Posture** to transform it into a retro mechanical Split-Flap, Amber LED, or OLED Matrix concourse board with a touch control deck.
